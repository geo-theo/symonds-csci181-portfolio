A GIS accessibility project that maps travel time and health access factors related to snakebite antivenom availability in India.

  • Mapped hospital and antivenom access locations.
  • Compared travel time with population exposure.
  • Highlighted areas where access may be limited.

A spatial statistics project that identifies neighborhoods with weaker public transportation access in Paris.

  • Used spatial statistics to find access patterns.

Madagascar Orographic Effect

A cartography and physical geography project mapping how terrain influences rainfall and environmental patterns in Madagascar.

  • Designed a clear visual explanation of the orographic effect.
